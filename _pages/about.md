I am a scientist at the [Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research (PIK)](https://www.mcc-berlin.net/) in Berlin.
I study large collections of text about climate change using Natural Language Processing (NLP).

I am currently working on developing tools and methods for the responsible application of AI to evidence synthesis in climate change and health.
